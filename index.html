Biography

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Nanjing University, advised by Prof. Haipeng Dai. I received my M.E. degree from Sichuan Normal University in 2023 under the supervision of Prof. Tang Liu, and my B.E. degree from the School of Mathematical Sciences at Sichuan Normal University in 2020.
